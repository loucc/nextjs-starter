# About SerenAI

SerenAI is your dedicated **AI emotional companion** — a gentle, always-available presence designed to support your mental wellness. Powered by advanced AI emotion recognition technology, SerenAI listens, understands, and responds with personalized emotional comfort, anytime you need it.

## What SerenAI Does

- 💙 **Professional Mood Healing** — science-informed techniques to help you process difficult feelings and recover your balance.
- 🧭 **Emotional Guidance** — thoughtful, non-judgmental conversations that help you understand what you're feeling and why.
- 🌿 **Stress Relief & Mental Relaxation** — guided calming exercises to ease tension and quiet a busy mind.
- 🌙 **Round-the-Clock Support** — 24/7 availability, because difficult moments don't keep business hours.

## Our Mission

Modern life brings anxiety, emotional burnout, and loneliness to more people than ever. We believe everyone deserves a safe space to be heard — without stigma, without waiting lists, without judgment. SerenAI combines the warmth of companionship with the precision of technology, delivering gentle, tech-driven mental wellness to calm your mind.
