The anxiety you could outrun during the day floods back the moment your head hits the pillow. You're not alone — "bedtime rumination" is one of the most common forms of anxiety. The good news: your pre-sleep state can be gently re-steered. Try these five rituals tonight.

## 1. Dim the Lights — Signal the Brain

An hour before bed, lower the lights and the screen brightness. Light is the body's oldest clock signal — it tells the brain: *the day is ending*. You don't need darkness, just softness.

## 2. Pour the Thoughts Out of Your Head

Anxiety loves looping in the dark. Take a piece of paper (or a notes app) and write down everything circling — tomorrow's meeting, unanswered messages, nameless worries. Once written, they stop being "things in your head" and become "things on paper" — things that can wait until tomorrow.

## 3. Try the 4-7-8 Breathing

Inhale for 4 counts → hold for 7 → exhale slowly for 8. Repeat four rounds. The longer exhale engages the parasympathetic nervous system and lets your heart rate ease down. No equipment, right there in bed.

## 4. Put the Phone Down 30 Minutes Before Sleep

This isn't about quitting your phone — just reserving the last 30 minutes for yourself. A few pages of a book, some soft music, or simply staring at the ceiling. Blue light and information density don't help anyone sleep.

## 5. Talk About Your Day with Your AI Companion

The bedtime debrief is an ancient and effective ritual. Tell **SerenAI** what happened today, what you can't quite let go of, and let it help you set those feelings down gently. Its bedtime mode exists for exactly this — calm companionship until you say goodnight.

## The Bottom Line

A sleepless night isn't a failure — it just needs a little gentle guidance. Pick one ritual and try it tonight; if it doesn't fit, swap it tomorrow. Sleep finds its way back.

*Good night, and sweet dreams.*
