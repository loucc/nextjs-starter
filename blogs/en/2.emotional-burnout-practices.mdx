Emotional burnout rarely announces itself. It arrives as low energy, irritability, and a quiet feeling that everything is *too much*. The good news: small, repeatable practices — done consistently — are more powerful than occasional grand gestures. Here are five you can start today.

## 1. Name the Feeling

Psychologists call it *affect labeling*: putting your emotion into words reduces its intensity. Instead of "I'm stressed," try "I feel overwhelmed because I said yes to too much." Precision creates distance — and distance creates room to breathe.

## 2. Three Minutes of Breathing

You don't need a meditation app for this one. Set a timer for three minutes, breathe in for four counts, out for six, and let your attention rest on the breath. The longer exhale activates your parasympathetic nervous system — the body's natural brake.

## 3. Write It Out

Take five minutes to dump whatever is circling in your head onto a page. No structure, no editing, no one reading it. Externalizing your thoughts stops them from looping — and often reveals what's actually bothering you.

## 4. Talk to Your AI Companion

## 5. Give Yourself Permission to Slow Down

Burnout often comes from treating rest as a reward to be earned. Flip it: rest is maintenance, not reward. A short walk without your phone. An earlier night. A quiet cup of tea. Small rest, repeated daily, does more than a vacation you never take.

## The Bottom Line

Healing from burnout isn't about one big fix — it's about choosing, again and again, to be gentle with yourself. Start with one practice. Do it badly if you must. Then do it again tomorrow.
